# 试玩验证报告

版本 0.2.0，Unity 6000.3.24f1；2026-09-15。

## 自动运行验证

以下为实际构建的 Mac 程序执行结果。小队专项通过真实寻路、碰撞和射线射击验证；完整对局由自动玩家参与，不代替真人手感评测。

### squad

结果：通过；32 项检查；状态 Assault；模拟 1.2 秒；第 1 波；生成 0；击杀 0；玩家击杀 0；小队击杀 0。

该专项末尾会真正重载场景，验证小队与经济重置，所以报告末尾的击杀/人数为新对局的零值；前置测试结果保留在 checks。

- Garrison and squad begin with separate rosters
- Hiring requires the command terminal or intermission
- The nearby command terminal pauses the battle
- Hiring creates one living squad member and deducts exactly 120
- Insufficient funds never create a squad member
- Squad cap rejects a fourth recruit without charging
- Recruiting never consumes an automatic garrison slot
- Squad movement freezes with the paused simulation
- Orders cannot change while the battle is paused
- All three followers route around the HQ and barracks
- Followers remain outside building collision volumes
- Followers cross the base and navigate around the bunker
- A living player can issue the hold-position order
- A held squad remains at its order location when the player moves away
- Follow resumes and all members traverse the west ramp
- Squad returns uphill through the east entrance without crossing walls
- Every squad member prioritizes the player's marked visible target
- Squad bullets deal real damage to the marked target instead of the nearer alternative
- Squad kills are counted and dead focus targets are cleared
- A squad casualty frees only its own slot
- Intermission recruitment fills the vacant slot at the normal price
- A dead player cannot command or recruit
- The player's death does not delete their squad
- Player rounds pass through squadmates without damage or obstruction
- A ray through the exposed core applies armor and the 1.65 weak-point multiplier
- A body shot uses armor reduction without a false weak-point bonus
- Firing consumes one round and produces recoverable recoil
- Recoil settles after firing stops
- Paused firing cannot spend ammunition
- Ignoring friendly units never allows shooting through the HQ
- A projectile whose owner is gone still collides with world geometry
- Restart clears the previous squad, orders, credits and battle state

## 修复记录

- 修正场景序列化仍使用旧的 80 开局补给，现为 160。
- 修正队员到达路径点前切角导致的坡道边缘卡住。
- 修正子弹来源被销毁后，把没有 Health 组件的墙体误当作来源而忽略的碰撞问题。
- 网页实际运行暴露了动态创建球体时 SphereCollider 被裁剪的问题，改为保留明确的类型引用。
- 浏览器快速解锁/再次锁定鼠标时可能拒绝请求，模板现处理该请求的 Promise 拒绝，让失去鼠标控制时按原流程暂停。

## 画面、浏览器与限制

- Mac 实际渲染了小队终端与战斗画面：`Verification/v0.2/terminal.png`、`battle.png`。
- Web 在本机内置 Chromium 浏览器实际完成加载、开局、E 雇佣并扣费、G 驻守、F 跟随、Esc 暂停和继续；最终构建没有再出现 SphereCollider 缺失错误。详见 `Verification/v0.2/web-browser.json`。
- 网页日志仍有未使用的 URP 辅助着色器不受支持提示和持久化接口弃用警告；画面正常渲染。内置 Chromium 的鼠标锁定拒绝已被单独处理，复测可继续游戏。
- 未在 Windows 真机、独立 Chrome / Safari 或手机上测试；未承诺特定帧率。
- 构建结果为 `Verification/v0.2/build-*.json`。Mac / Web 零错误零警告；Windows 零错误，18 条未使用的光线追踪着色器编译警告。
