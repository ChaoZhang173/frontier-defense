# 试玩验证报告

版本 0.1.0，Unity 6000.3.24f1；2026-09-15。

## mechanics
结果：通过；状态 Defeat；模拟 6.3 秒；第 1 波；生成 3；击杀 1；玩家击杀 0。

- HQ, barracks, bunker, player and four initial defenders exist
- Five wave definitions contain exactly 198 invaders
- Production refuses units beyond its cap
- Friendly rifle damage cannot harm the base
- Insufficient credits do not purchase or go negative
- Player upgrade has a hard level cap
- Maxed upgrades never charge credits
- Repairs clamp at maximum health
- Full-health repair never charges credits
- HQ collider blocks rifle fire at an enemy behind it
- Purchases are disabled during an assault
- An empty field does not finish a wave with pending spawns
- Pause freezes simulation time
- Player respawns after six seconds while the battle continues
- Respawn restores a complete magazine
- Reload completes after its timer
- Destroying the command center ends the mission
